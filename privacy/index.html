Simple Video Editor – By Qierah

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Privacy at a glance

Your videos, photos, audio, recordings and projects are edited on your computer. The app does not automatically upload them to Qierah or OpenAI. It has no built-in advertising, Qierah analytics or automatic crash-reporting service.

Microsoft handles Store purchases and licence checks. An optional, user-initiated download obtains the video export codec from Cisco. If you email support, your message passes through Porkbun to Outlook. Local recovery files help protect unfinished work and have no fixed expiry.

Who is responsible

Qierah is an independent Australian software publisher. We develop and publish software applications. This policy covers Simple Video Editor – By Qierah for Windows and support for that app. For privacy questions, requests or complaints, email support@qierah.com.

Microsoft, Cisco and email providers also handle information when you use their services. Their own privacy notices explain their practices. Website visits are explained separately below.

Your media and editing

The app accesses the videos, photos, audio and project files you choose, and the text and editing instructions you enter, to provide editing, previews and exports. These activities take place locally using Windows media components and the app’s local rendering and audio tools.

A saved project contains editing instructions, text, timings and references to source files; it is not a copy or backup of the original media. Included stickers are pre-generated image files. Using them does not contact OpenAI or require an OpenAI account.

The app has no Qierah account or built-in service for uploading your media or projects. If you choose a network drive or a folder backed up or synchronised by OneDrive or another service, that service may transfer files according to your settings.

Microphone and voice recordings

The app starts recording when you choose Record Voice. Windows microphone access settings and your input device affect whether recording is available. Switching to another window does not itself stop recording. Use Stop Recording to finish and save a WAV recording locally. You can manage access in Windows Settings under Privacy & security > Microphone (or Privacy > Microphone on Windows 10), including the desktop-app setting where applicable.

The app uses a local staging file while saving a recording, then moves a playable recording to Documents/Qierah Projects/Recorded Voice. A failed save or move can leave a partial or playable file in local staging storage. Removing a voice item from the timeline does not delete its recording file. The app does not provide cloud transcription or automatically send recordings to support.

Local storage, caches and diagnostics

Suggested save locations are Documents/Qierah Projects for project files and Videos/Qierah for exports. Save dialogues let you choose destinations. Voice recordings use the Recorded Voice folder described above.

The Microsoft Store version uses its Windows application storage, normally under %LOCALAPPDATA%/Packages/Qierah.QierahSimpleVideoEditor_f8skmscgtr1je/LocalState, for preferences, recovery copies, codec settings and downloads, audio caches, recording staging files and any local diagnostics. Windows may redirect user folders.

Preview and export caches can contain decoded audio, mixed audio and timing information. Error details and project files can contain filenames and full paths, including a Windows username. Safe saves retain separately named backups of earlier project or settings files. An interrupted save can leave a pending file; a failed or interrupted export can leave a partial MP4 in the export destination.

There is no fixed automatic cleanup period for these backups, caches, diagnostic files or failed-operation remnants in this release. They can remain until you remove them, Windows removes app storage, or another cleanup process acts. The app does not automatically transmit diagnostic files to Qierah.

Automatic project recovery

The app normally writes a separate recovery copy locally about one second after project edits. Recovery contains the project’s editing instructions, entered text, media filenames and paths, the source project name/path, the last preview position and the recovery save time. It does not copy the source media itself or upload the recovery data.

Each unfinished session can retain the latest and previous committed recovery versions in its own recovery folder within app storage. If the latest is unreadable, the app can offer the earlier readable version. A session still in use by another editor window is excluded from the recovery list.

A successful manual save clears the current session’s recovery copies where cleanup succeeds. Normal closing also attempts to clear that session, including when you choose not to save. If cleanup fails, files may remain; a normally closed session may be marked so it is not offered for recovery.

Unfinished recovery copies left after an unexpected stop or after switching away from unsaved work have no fixed expiry. Keep for Later leaves them available. Discard Copy deletes the selected recovery copies without deleting saved projects or original media. Restoring transfers the project into the current session and clears the older copies after a successful transfer; use Save Project to keep your own project file.

Recovery needs the original media to remain available. App reset or removal, manual cleanup and storage failure can remove recovery files. Recovery is not an independent backup.

Microsoft Store purchases, trials and licence checks

The Store version asks Windows.Services.Store for the app’s licence status at startup and during use. It uses purchase/trial status and, for a trial, its expiry time to decide whether editing is available. Microsoft’s service may use a cached licence; offline availability depends on the licence it returns.

Choosing the Microsoft Store offer opens Microsoft’s purchase process after confirmation. Microsoft handles account sign-in and payment. The editor does not collect your Microsoft account password or payment-card details. The licence check does not send your media or projects to Qierah.

Microsoft separately processes Store, account, transaction and service information under the Microsoft Privacy Statement: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/privacystatement.

Cisco OpenH264 and other connections

Video export uses the OpenH264 Video Codec provided by Cisco Systems, Inc. Choosing Download codec directly from Cisco requests the codec over HTTPS from ciscobinary.openh264.org. The app checks the download’s hashes and Windows signature validation before enabling it. The codec and its path setting are stored locally.

The download exposes connection information, including your IP address and the requested download, to Cisco and its delivery infrastructure. Windows certificate and revocation checks may also contact certificate services. No media or project content is included in the app’s codec download request. See Cisco’s Privacy Statement: https://www.cisco.com/site/us/en/about/legal/privacy.html.

The app has no built-in Qierah usage tracking, advertising or automatic crash uploads. Windows and Microsoft Store can process diagnostics and other information separately under their own settings and notices. Opening an external link uses your browser or email application and that service’s practices apply.

Support email and retention

If you contact support@qierah.com, Qierah receives your email address, message and any name, attachments, screenshots, diagnostic details or file paths you choose to send. This information is used to respond, troubleshoot and deal with related complaints. Without enough information, it may not be possible to resolve your request. Please send only what is needed and remove unrelated personal information from logs or images.

Incoming support email is forwarded through Porkbun to an Outlook inbox provided by Microsoft. Qierah handles support correspondence through these services. Porkbun and Microsoft process message content and related delivery information to provide their services. See Porkbun’s Privacy Policy at https://porkbun.com/legal/agreement/privacy_policy and the Microsoft Privacy Statement linked above.

Our support retention policy is to keep routine correspondence while the matter is open and for 12 months after resolution, then delete it. This is a manual retention practice, not an automatic deletion feature of the app. Records needed for a legal obligation or an unresolved dispute may be kept longer while that need continues. Provider backups and service logs follow the provider’s own retention arrangements; deleting a message does not guarantee immediate removal of all provider copies.

Support information may be processed outside Australia. Porkbun states that it processes personal data in the United States. Microsoft states that it may process information in your region, the United States and other countries where it or its service providers operate. The Outlook service arrangements determine the locations used for this inbox; information is not guaranteed to remain in Australia. Their privacy notices explain international processing.

Security, deletion and your choices

The app does not add its own encryption or password protection to projects, recordings, recovery files, caches or diagnostics. Local protection depends on your Windows account, device security, folder permissions and any disk encryption you enable. Other people or software with access to those files may be able to read them.

You can choose which media to open, stop recording, manage Windows microphone access and delete saved files using Windows. Close the app before manually removing its caches or recovery storage, and keep any work you need first. Safe-save backups and separately saved media may need to be deleted individually. Removing an item from a timeline does not erase its source file.

Windows app reset or uninstall can remove application storage but should not be relied on to erase all separately saved projects, recordings, exports, backups or cloud copies. Qierah cannot remotely access, recover or delete files on your computer through this app. Support email is held separately from your local files.

Privacy requests and complaints

Email support@qierah.com to ask what personal information Qierah holds about you, request access or correction, ask for deletion, or make a privacy complaint. Explain your request and provide enough information to locate the relevant correspondence. We may need to verify your identity before disclosing or changing information; do not send passwords or identity documents unless specifically needed and requested.

Requests are considered under applicable law. Access or deletion may be limited where records must be retained or another lawful restriction applies. For information held independently by Microsoft, Cisco, Porkbun or another provider, use that provider’s privacy controls or contact details.

Please raise complaints with Qierah first. Qierah will review your concerns, seek any necessary details and respond with the outcome and any proposed steps. If the Australian Privacy Act applies to the matter and you are dissatisfied with the response, or have not received a response within 30 days, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC), subject to its jurisdiction and complaint requirements: https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-complaints. Depending on your location, other privacy rights or complaint avenues may also apply.

Visiting the Qierah website

The website is separate from the Windows editor and is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security and processes technical request information under its Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

This privacy page does not load analytics scripts or set analytics cookies. Other pages on qierah.com offer optional Google Analytics after you accept website analytics. That website tracking is not included in the editor. See the separate website privacy policy at https://qierah.com/privacy-policy.html for website analytics details and preference controls.
