Qierah Privacy Policy

Qierah is a personal desktop application used to create, edit and publish original video content to social media accounts authorised by the user.

Qierah may access account information and permissions required to publish content to connected services including TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Instagram.

Qierah does not sell personal information or provide personal data to advertisers. Account credentials and access tokens are used only to provide requested publishing functions.

Users may revoke access to connected social media accounts at any time through the relevant platform's account settings.

Qierah only publishes content when instructed by the authorised user.
