Privacy Policy

Clear information for the website and our software in development.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who this policy covers

Qierah is the publishing name of an independent Australian software developer creating Windows applications and other digital products. This policy explains this website, contact and support enquiries, and the current development scope of our applications. Products still in development are not available for public download here.

This website

This is an informational website hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no sign-in or payment form and does not ask for access to your microphone, files or social media accounts. Optional Google Analytics tracking is available only if you accept it. Your analytics preference is saved in your browser's local storage.

GitHub processes technical information associated with hosting and requests, such as IP addresses and browser information, under its Privacy Statement. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own data practices.

Optional Google Analytics

With your permission, this website uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-ZJ1F5EQEMG) to understand website visits and page views. Google may set cookies and process usage, device and network information under its own policies. Analytics is not loaded if you decline. We do not use Google Analytics for advertising personalisation.

You can change your choice at any time: Manage analytics preferences. Declining future analytics stops the website from loading analytics on subsequent pages; it does not retroactively erase data already collected by Google. See Google's Privacy Policy for further information.

Glitter Pix – Photo Editor By Qierah

Glitter Pix is a Windows photo editor for children and families. Photos, background removal and editing are processed on your computer. The app has no Qierah account requirement, advertising, analytics, telemetry, public sharing feed, chat, built-in photo-upload service or live generative-image service. The optional website analytics described above do not run inside Glitter Pix. The app does not request camera, microphone or location permission.

Photos, projects and personal stickers

Opening a photo reads the local file you choose. Saving a finished photo, editable project or PNG sticker copy writes to the location you choose. Editable .qphoto projects include the images needed to reopen them and may retain original local file paths to help prevent accidental overwriting. Those images and paths stay in the project and are not sent to Qierah or to Microsoft Store by the app. Finished-photo exports omit original EXIF metadata. Files you save in a cloud-synchronised folder may be synced by that provider according to your device settings.

Favourites, recently used sticker identifiers, welcome-guide choices, personal sticker PNGs and automatic recovery projects are stored locally. The usual app data location is %LOCALAPPDATA%\GlitterPix; Windows may manage or redirect this location for packaged installations. Personal stickers are kept in its my-stickers folder and recovery projects in its recovery folder. These files can include identifiable people and the photos needed to restore unfinished work. The app can also read earlier Qierah Photo Editor recovery files and migrate favourites from the earlier local data folder.

The app normally removes its own recovery file after a normal close. Saved projects, exported pictures and personal stickers are not automatically deleted when you close a creation. Uninstalling the app may not remove files you saved elsewhere. You can remove local files using Windows File Explorer; keep a backup of creations you want before deleting files or uninstalling. Glitter Pix does not add passwords or encryption to project or personal-sticker files, so use your Windows account and device controls to protect them.

Local background removal and decorative artwork

Background removal uses a bundled local model, with no photograph upload or model download during use. Original photo data can remain embedded in an editable project so the background can be restored. Static decorative artwork includes OpenAI-generated illustrations; no photo is sent to OpenAI by Glitter Pix. Face stickers are manually placed decorations: the app does not identify people or create face-recognition profiles.

Microsoft Store purchases

Microsoft Store handles optional purchases, product prices, licence checks and restoration through Microsoft's account and licensing services. Product lookup, purchase and restoration may require an internet connection; an operating-system-cached licence may support offline verification when available. Glitter Pix sends the Store helper purchase/licensing information, not your photos, stickers, project contents or original file paths. Microsoft's account, payment and Store services have their own privacy practices.

Optional external links and support

The sticker guide includes links to an external animation website. A website opens only when you select its link. Glitter Pix does not attach or upload your photos, stickers or projects when opening a link, and it does not perform GIF conversion itself. If you later choose files on an external website, that service may upload them or offer local processing according to its settings and privacy policy. Your browser, Windows and other services have their own data practices.

Ask a parent or guardian before purchasing add-ons or sharing photos outside the app. For privacy or support questions, contact info@qierah.com or visit Support. Please do not send children's photographs or editable projects unless support specifically needs them and you choose to provide them. The contact, retention and privacy-request information below also applies to information you deliberately send for Glitter Pix support.

Simple Video Editor – By Qierah

The Windows desktop editor processes selected videos, photos, audio, text and overlays locally. Projects refer to media files on your device, and exports are saved to a location you choose. Voice recording uses the microphone when you start a recording, subject to Windows microphone permissions, and saves recordings on your device.

The current editor does not include AI processing, a Qierah account, social media publishing or a built-in upload of your projects to Qierah. It has no built-in Qierah advertising or analytics service. Keep your own backups. Closing a project or removing a recording from the timeline does not necessarily delete the original media or saved recording; you control those files through your device.

This is a brief overview of the Windows editor. The video editor has a separate, detailed app-specific privacy policy explaining its current data handling, Microsoft Store licensing, optional codec download and local recovery files. Files saved in a folder managed by a cloud-sync service may be synced by that service according to your device settings.

Qierah AI Helper and future software

Qierah AI Helper is a separate AI-assisted software development project, currently in development. No assistant chat or file upload is provided by this website. AI-assisted features can involve external providers. Product-specific information about the providers used, data sent, storage, retention and available controls will be provided before a public release or service requiring that processing.

Custom software projects can have different data needs. Any proposed access to personal or confidential information, external AI processing or hosted storage must be discussed as part of the project’s scope before that processing begins. This website is not an invitation to submit confidential project files.

Connected services and social accounts

Social account integrations belong to the separate Qierah AI development project, not the Very Simple Video Editor. A supported connection requires the account holder’s authorisation through the relevant platform. Depending on the enabled feature, account identifiers, permissions, access tokens and content selected for publishing may be processed for the requested action.

Website visits do not grant that access or authorise publishing. Access can be revoked in the relevant platform’s account settings. Revoking access does not automatically remove content already published or data held by that platform. Public availability of an integration is not promised on this website.

Contact and support information

If you contact Qierah, the information you choose to provide is used to understand and respond to your enquiry or resolve a support issue. Please provide only what is needed. Support information may include your chosen contact details, product version, a description of a problem and any diagnostic material you deliberately share.

Email Qierah at info@qierah.com for support, business enquiries or privacy requests. Email messages include the sender’s address, message content and any attachments you choose to send. Messages sent to this address are forwarded by Porkbun to the business inbox. Porkbun processes forwarding data under its Privacy Policy; Microsoft handles the destination email service under its Privacy Statement. Clicking an email link opens your configured email application; it does not automatically send a message.

GitHub Issues remains an option for public, non-sensitive feedback. Your GitHub profile and anything you post there can be visible publicly. Do not post passwords, access tokens, personal documents, private recordings or confidential files. GitHub controls account and issue data under its own privacy terms.

Retention, choices and privacy requests

You control the local project, media, export and recording files on your device. Information voluntarily sent for an enquiry is kept as needed to respond, resolve the matter and meet applicable record-keeping obligations; no fixed automatic deletion period is promised.

For questions about your information, or to request access, correction or deletion where applicable, email info@qierah.com or see Contact & Support. Include only the information needed to explain your request, and do not send passwords or access tokens. Information held by Microsoft, GitHub or another third-party service is also subject to that service’s controls and policies.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when the website or product data handling changes. Check the date above and any product-specific notice before using a released application or connected service.
