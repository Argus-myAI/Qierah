Social Media Video Editor privacy

Last updated: 7 November 2026. Applies specifically to Free Social Media Video Editor - By Qierah for Windows.

This application is in development. Social publishing features depend on completed platform configuration, review and availability in your installed build. This page does not claim platform approval or successful live publishing.

This preview processes selected photos, video, audio, text and projects locally. Format presets do not connect social accounts or publish videos. No Qierah account is required. The optional YouTube connection described below is separate from editing and is available only in a build with completed Google registration.

OPTIONAL YOUTUBE UPLOADS If you select Connect in Creator tools > YouTube, the app opens Google's sign-in/consent page in your system browser. Google handles your password. YouTube upload permission allows the selected video/metadata to be sent to your channel; read permission is used to display/confirm the connected channel. No media is uploaded just by connecting. After you choose an exported video, title, description/tags, visibility, made-for-kids audience and optional altered/synthetic-content declaration, a separate confirmation is required before uploading. The selected file and these details then go directly from your PC to Google's YouTube services, not to Qierah. Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and YouTube's Terms (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) apply.

Google access/refresh tokens, the connected channel ID/name and resumable-upload records (including local file path, SHA-256 fingerprint, session address and selected metadata) are stored under the app's connections folder, encrypted using your Windows-user DPAPI protection. They are not saved inside projects or sent to Qierah. Pausing can leave an encrypted resume record and data already received by YouTube. Disconnect requests Google revocation and removes local connection/resume records; it does not delete videos already uploaded. Forget pending upload removes local resume details only. If revocation cannot be confirmed, use https://myaccount.google.com/connections to remove access yourself. YouTube authorization does not authorize Facebook, Instagram or TikTok; those integrations require separate platform setup and consent.

When a configured build connects to Meta, your system browser handles Facebook sign-in and consent. Qierah does not receive your Meta password. Qierah's hosted connection service exchanges the authorization code, checks granted permissions and discovers authorized Facebook Pages and linked professional Instagram accounts. Connecting alone sends no video. Personal Facebook profiles are not publishing destinations in this workflow.

The service encrypts Meta tokens, account identifiers/permissions and temporary desktop handoff results. Windows stores its separate per-user broker credential and any saved publishing job using Windows-user DPAPI protection. Local resume data includes the selected file path, SHA-256 fingerprint, destination and confirmed metadata. These records are separate from your editing project. Server job records contain the destination, file fingerprint/size and confirmed metadata; the local file path is not needed by the server.

If publishing is enabled in a future configured build, you must select the destination, video, caption and supported Page/account audience and confirm before transfer. Timeline export uses that confirmation, then exports and uploads automatically after you choose export settings and a save location. The Meta workflow sends the approved MP4 through Qierah's Cloudflare-hosted service to Meta, unlike the direct-to-Google YouTube workflow above. The initial staging transport is limited to 16 MiB and buffers media transiently for fingerprint verification and relay; it does not intentionally retain a server-side video file. It does not send your full editing project. A transfer receipt or processing result is not necessarily confirmed publication.

Authorization sessions expire after ten minutes; hourly cleanup may retain expired encrypted records for roughly another hour. Encrypted Meta connection data is retained for no longer than the shorter of the provider's token lifetime and thirty days, plus cleanup delay. Job eligibility expires after twenty-four hours; job/resume safeguards remain while their connection exists so an expired or uncertain request cannot silently be duplicated. Disconnect and validated Meta removal callbacks clear the affected Qierah connection, jobs and handoff records. Minimal keyed replay safeguards and opaque deletion-completion receipts expire after eight days. Hosting providers may process network/request metadata under their own policies; application modules do not log OAuth codes, tokens or video contents.

Disconnect attempts Meta revocation and reports whether it was confirmed. Cancelling or forgetting a local job does not undo data already received by Meta or delete published posts. An interrupted publishing request may still complete; check the saved job and destination account before uploading again. You can also remove Qierah through Facebook's Business integrations settings at https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools. Meta's applicable terms and privacy policies govern its processing. Public app-specific disclosures and release/review declarations must be completed before general availability.

Creator tools use local processing. Automatic English captions use the bundled Whisper speech model on your PC; no speech, transcript or project is sent to an AI service. The model is included with the application, so normal caption generation needs no model download or API key. Audio is converted into temporary local audio/cache files for transcription. Caption text and timing are saved in your project and any SRT file you export. Review generated text before sharing it.

Creator templates save your chosen font, colour, logo reference, social handle and intro/outro wording in the app's local data folder. Imported logo copies, generated freeze frames and title-card images are kept in Documents/Qierah Social Projects/Creator assets during normal use. Saved project files reference these images and other source media; they are not self-contained media bundles. Keep these assets with your backups. Noise filtering, audio waveforms and music ducking also run locally. Their caches may contain audio derived from your selected files. Windows account security applies; the app does not add separate encryption to these files. Cloud-synchronised folders may be uploaded by your chosen sync provider according to its settings.

The Windows preview stores settings, recovery data and caches in your Windows-user local application-data folder under Qierah/SocialVideoEditor. Saved projects can reference selected files and local paths; do not share a project expecting those references to be private or to include every original media file. Exports and projects use your chosen destinations. Recovery copies help recover unsaved changes. Removing the program does not automatically delete your own projects, videos or every recovery/cache file.

Microsoft Store handles any configured purchase/licence request using your Microsoft account. The free app licence does not grant ad removal. No payment is enabled while this new product's configuration is missing. Optional Cisco codec download is obtained directly from Cisco; see Codec & licensing and the complete included licence.

No advertising SDK is linked or serving ads in this development preview. The planned free release will contain advertising, with a one-time ad-removal purchase. Liftoff Monetize is a candidate only. Before enabling ads, this information and the public privacy policy must name the actual provider, collected/shared data, retention, consent/privacy controls, advertising ID treatment and age/audience settings. This preview document is not a final privacy disclosure for an ad-enabled release.

TikTok development and planned publishing

The TikTok developer app and restricted test sandbox have been registered. The customer-facing TikTok integration is still being completed; registration does not authorize public publishing. No TikTok account is connected merely by installing or editing with the app.

The intended optional workflow opens TikTok sign-in and consent in your system browser. TikTok handles your password. Qierah's hosted service will exchange the authorization code and protect provider tokens on the server. The app will display current creator information and privacy choices, require your account/video/settings selection and required music or commercial-content confirmations, and transfer only the video you approve. The intended file-transfer path sends the approved MP4 directly from your PC to TikTok using a limited upload address. The planned service will retain encrypted connection and publishing-status data. The exact enabled data handling, retention and removal controls will be updated here before a customer release; current local protocol tests do not establish that this workflow is available.

Privacy requests and support

Contact support@qierah.com to request access, correction or deletion of personal information Qierah holds about you, or to raise a privacy concern. Provide enough information to identify the request; do not send passwords or access tokens. Qierah may need to verify account ownership before acting. Removing a connection does not remove posts held by a social platform. Use the platform's account and content controls for those posts. See also Qierah's website privacy policy.
