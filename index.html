QIERAH is an AI-assisted desktop content creation and publishing application designed for creator workflows.

It brings video planning, media creation, editing and social publishing together in one workflow, allowing users to review and approve their content before publishing it to authorised social media accounts.

About QIERAH

QIERAH Productions develops tools that assist creators with the production and management of original educational and entertainment video content.

QIERAH combines different stages of video production into a single desktop workflow, from developing a project through to preparing and publishing the finished content.

Content Creation Workflow

Plan

Organise video projects, narration, scenes and other production information before content is created.

Create & Edit

Prepare original video content using media, narration and editing tools within the QIERAH production workflow.

Review & Publish

Review completed content and choose when and where authorised content is published.

Social Media Publishing

QIERAH can connect to supported social platforms using their authorised account and API systems. Users remain in control of account authorisation and publishing actions.

TikTok Integration

QIERAH integrates with TikTok to provide an authorised publishing workflow for users.

Account Authorisation

Users authorise access to their TikTok account through TikTok's authentication process.

User-Controlled Publishing

Content is prepared and reviewed before the user chooses to publish it to their authorised account.

Platform Permissions

QIERAH only requests platform access required for the supported publishing functions being used.

Privacy & User Control

QIERAH is designed around user-controlled account access and publishing. Users choose which supported accounts they authorise and control the content they submit for publishing.
