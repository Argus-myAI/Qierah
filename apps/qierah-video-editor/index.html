Qierah Video Editor

Make social videos on your iPhone with a timeline, overlays, voice recording and captions.

View on App Store

Features

  • Trim and split clips, change their speed, and arrange them on a timeline.
  • Add photos, text and stickers, and mix music and narration with the original audio.
  • Edit captions manually, import or share SRT files, or generate English captions when on-device recognition is available.
  • Save projects and share MP4 exports up to 4K. Requires iOS 17 or later.
