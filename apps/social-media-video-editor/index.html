Made for your social videos

From a first clip to a finished MP4.

Choose portrait, landscape, square or 4:5 to suit your story. Save an editable project so you can return to it later.

  • Trim, split and arrange videos and photos on a multitrack timeline.
  • Add music, microphone narration, editable text and stickers.
  • Generate English captions locally, edit the words, and import or export SRT subtitles.
  • Adjust framing, speed, transitions, fades and audio levels.
  • Create beginning and end screens with your own wording and logo.
  • Export MP4 in 720p, Full HD or 4K, with frame-rate and quality options.
  • Save projects with Undo, Redo and available autosave recovery.

Before you start

Local editing does not require a Qierah or social-media account. English speech recognition can make mistakes, so review captions before sharing.

H.264 export requires a separate Cisco OpenH264 codec download through the app’s Codec & licensing instructions. Internet access is needed for that download and optional online features. Higher export settings need more time, memory and disk space.

Keep your original media and generated project assets in their saved locations. Project files reference your media rather than containing it all.

Sharing your finished video

Export an MP4 and upload it using your preferred platform’s website or app. Direct Facebook, Instagram and TikTok uploading is currently disabled. Qierah is independently developed and is not endorsed by those platforms.
