Qierah Video Editor for iPhone — Privacy

Updated 13 November 2026. This notice applies to the iPhone app, not the separate Windows edition. Contact Qierah at info@qierah.com.

Your editing stays on your device

Videos, photos, text, stickers, imported music, voice recordings, projects and exports are processed locally. Qierah does not run an account system or upload your project media to its servers. Automatic English captions use on-device speech recognition when available; the app does not send your video for server transcription. You choose where to share an export using Apple’s share sheet.

Permissions and storage

You choose media using Apple’s pickers. Microphone access is requested when you record voice; photo-library add access is used when you save an export to Photos. Imported copies and saved/recovery projects remain in the app’s storage until you remove them or uninstall the app. Exported copies, shared copies and device/cloud backups are controlled separately by you and your chosen services. Keep copies of important projects and media.

Advertising

Starting with version 1.0.2, on first launch, the editor asks whether you are 18 or older and agree to personalized advertising. Start.io starts only after that agreement and Apple tracking authorization. If you decline either choice, the editor displays a Qierah promotion bundled with the app. Displaying that promotion makes no ad request and does not track you. If you tap it, it opens the Qierah website, which has its own privacy notice. Your choice does not affect editing or export.

Start.io and its partners may process device identifiers, IP address and network information, location information, advertising/product interactions, advertising data, other SDK data, crashes and diagnostics. The bundled Start.io 4.14.0 privacy declaration includes precise and coarse location, device ID, product interaction, advertising data, other data, crash data and other diagnostic data. The editor does not request GPS permission or provide your media, captions, name, email, age or gender to the SDK. The provider may derive location from network information. Location and device identifiers may be used for tracking as described by Start.io, including association with data from other apps or services.

Read the provider’s privacy policy, services terms and data partner list for its processing, recipients, retention, international transfers and rights procedures. Qierah does not operate these partner systems or receive your raw advertising profile.

Your advertising choices

Version 1.0.2 remembers your advertising choice on this device. You can switch to Qierah promotions under Advertising privacy or withdraw Apple tracking permission in iPhone Settings. The editor then stops requesting Start.io ads and shows a bundled Qierah promotion. The Start.io SDK has no public shutdown method. Restart the app to end any previously initialized SDK session. The app sends Start.io a restriction on sale and sharing. For access, deletion or sale and sharing requests about data held by Start.io, use the rights contacts and controls in its privacy policy.

Earlier versions

Versions 1.0 and 1.0.1 request non-personalized Start.io ads after adult consent and Apple tracking authorization. If you decline, no banner appears. In 1.0, use Turn ads off to remove Start.io banners. In 1.0.1, use Advertising privacy to change your choice. You can also withdraw tracking permission in iPhone Settings.

Support and changes

If you email support, your message and any attachments are handled to answer your request. Please do not send private project media unless you choose to share it for support.
