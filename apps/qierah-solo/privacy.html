Qierah Solo Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Qierah Solo — Business Manager for Windows.

Last updated 20 November 2026

Your business records

Qierah Solo processes and stores the records you enter on your Windows computer. These can include business details and payment instructions, customer contact details, quotes, invoices and recorded payments, jobs and private notes, checklists and costs, expenses and receipt files, mileage records, imported bank transactions, recurring invoice schedules and generation history.

The app uses these records to help you manage your business and prepare documents and reports. It does not upload them to Qierah. Qierah Solo contains no advertising or app analytics service and requires no Qierah account.

Local storage and backups

The working SQLite database, daily SQLite backups, restore safety copies and exported documents contain readable business information. The usual workspace location is %LOCALAPPDATA%\Qierah\Solo. Windows may manage or redirect app storage for packaged installations. Access depends on the protection and permissions of your Windows device and chosen storage locations.

Optional .qbackup files encrypt backup contents using a password you choose. Qierah does not hold that password and cannot reset it. Encrypted backup and restore operations use temporary local copies, which are deleted when the operation finishes. Keep your password somewhere safe so you can restore your records.

If you save files in a folder managed by OneDrive or another sync service, that service may copy them according to your settings and its policies. Fictional demonstration records use a separate workspace.

Private job notes

Private job notes stay out of customer quotes, invoices and receipts, ordinary job CSV exports, calendar exports and search results. They remain in the local workspace and complete backups. Anyone who can access an unencrypted workspace or backup may be able to read those notes.

Receipt photographs and PDFs

Attached receipt photographs and PDFs are copied into the workspace and included in complete backups. They are not uploaded to Qierah or processed by an external receipt service. The app can preview images locally. To view a PDF, you choose where to save a copy and the app opens your installed PDF viewer.

Removing an attachment from an expense does not remove the original source file, exported copies or earlier backups. You control those copies separately.

Bank transactions and recurring invoices

Bank CSV processing runs on your computer. The app stores account labels, transaction dates, descriptions, amounts, classifications, your notes and confirmed links to business records. This workflow does not ask for bank-login credentials or contact a bank-feed provider.

Recurring invoice schedules and their generation history remain local. Invoice drafts are generated when you request them. The app does not automatically email or issue recurring invoices while it is closed.

Microsoft Store services

The Microsoft Store edition asks Microsoft for product, purchase and licence information. Product lookup, purchase, restoration and licence checks may require an internet connection. These requests do not upload your business records. Microsoft processes Store account, payment and licensing information under its Privacy Statement. Qierah does not receive your payment card details through the app.

Your copies and choices

You control copies you print, export or share. Use the app's available controls to manage records and Windows file controls to manage workspace files and backups. Archiving a record keeps it in the workspace. Uninstalling the app may leave records or copies saved elsewhere. Keep the records you need before deleting workspace files or backups.

Support and privacy requests

If you email Qierah, we use your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide to respond and resolve your enquiry. Support information is kept as needed to handle the enquiry and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Please send only what is needed and keep passwords and confidential customer records out of your message unless you deliberately choose to provide material needed for support.

For support, privacy questions or requests about information you have sent to Qierah, email support@qierah.com. The app keeps your business workspace locally, so Qierah cannot remotely access or delete those local records.

Website hosting, optional website analytics and support-service information are explained in the general Qierah privacy policy. The website analytics described there are separate from the app. We publish updates to this app policy on this page.
