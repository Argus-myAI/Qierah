Qierah Solo for iPhone Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Qierah Solo: Business Manager for iPhone. The Windows edition has a separate policy.

Last updated 25 November 2026

Your business records stay on your device

Qierah Solo stores the business records you enter in its local iPhone workspace. Records can include business details and payment instructions, customer contact details, quotes, invoices and recorded payments, jobs and private notes, checklists and costs, expenses and receipts, mileage, imported bank transactions and recurring invoice schedules.

The app uses these records to prepare your documents and reports. It does not upload them to Qierah. It requires no Qierah account and contains no advertising, tracking or app analytics service.

Receipt photographs and selected files

The app requests Camera access when you choose to take a receipt photo. You can also select an existing image using Apple's photo picker, or choose a PNG, JPEG or PDF file. A receipt photograph or selected attachment is copied into the expense when you save it. Taking a photo does not automatically save it to your Photos library.

Receipt photos are stored locally and included in complete backups. Photos chosen through the photo picker and camera captures are stored as JPEG copies. Directly imported PNG, JPEG and PDF files retain their original file contents. No external receipt scanning or OCR service receives these files. You enter receipt amounts yourself.

Removing an attachment from an expense does not remove its source file, exported copies or earlier backups. You control those copies separately. Camera access can be changed in iOS Settings.

Local protection, backups and sharing

The working workspace uses iOS complete file protection and atomic saves with safety copies. Device access and protection still depend on your iPhone and its settings. Ordinary exported JSON backups and documents contain readable business information. Optional password backups use AES-256-GCM encryption with a key derived from your password. Qierah does not receive or store your password and cannot recover it.

When you export, print or share a file, you choose its destination using iOS controls. A destination such as iCloud Drive or another cloud provider may sync it according to that service's settings and policies. Device backups may also include app data according to your Apple settings. The app does not provide its own cloud sync service.

Private job notes are excluded from customer PDFs, ordinary job CSV, calendar exports and app search. They remain in your workspace and complete backups. Anyone with access to a readable backup may be able to read those notes.

The demonstration business uses separate temporary fictional records. Windows and iPhone editions do not automatically sync and use different backup formats.

Bank CSV files and recurring invoices

Bank CSV mapping, preview and confirmed matching run locally. The app does not ask for bank-login credentials or connect to a bank-feed provider. Imported account labels, dates, descriptions, amounts, classifications, notes and confirmed links stay in the workspace.

Recurring schedules create invoice drafts only when you request generation. The app does not email, issue or generate recurring invoices in the background while closed.

Apple purchases and platform services

Apple handles subscription checkout, billing and the Apple Account used for purchases. The app uses StoreKit to request product information, verify subscription access and restore purchases. These requests do not send your business workspace to Qierah. Qierah does not receive your payment card details through the app. Apple may provide sales reports and platform diagnostics under its services and your device settings. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Apple and Microsoft purchases are separate. Apple subscriptions can be managed or cancelled through your Apple Account. The app uses Apple's Standard End User License Agreement.

Your records and choices

You control the records entered, the permissions granted and the files you export or share. Archiving a record keeps it in the workspace. Use iOS storage controls to manage app data and manage separately any exported files, cloud copies and backups. Keep the records you need before deleting app data or backups. Qierah cannot remotely access, correct or delete the workspace stored on your device.

Support and privacy requests

If you email Qierah, we use your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send to respond and resolve your enquiry. Support information is retained as needed to handle the enquiry and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Please send only what is needed and avoid passwords or confidential customer records.

For support or privacy requests about information you have sent to Qierah, email support@qierah.com.

Website hosting and optional website analytics are covered by the general Qierah privacy policy. Website analytics are separate from this iPhone app. Updates to this policy will be published on this page.
