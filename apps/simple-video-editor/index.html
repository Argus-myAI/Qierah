Qierah’s take

A closer look

A publisher’s overview based on the app’s features, written by Qierah. This is our own assessment.

Simple Video Editor’s appeal is the combination of six editing tracks and familiar tasks: arranging clips, adding pictures and recording a voiceover. Separate audio volume controls help you balance narration with other sound. Landscape, portrait and square exports make it a practical choice for different ways of sharing a short video.

Who it suits

A good fit for everyday video projects, family memories, birthday videos, photo sequences and short social media clips.

Things to consider

Allow time for the separate Cisco OpenH264 codec download before your first export. Internet access is needed for that download and Microsoft Store licensing. Media compatibility depends on the Windows components installed on your PC. Keep original media files available when reopening saved projects.

Before your first MP4 export

Export requires a separate, user-initiated download of the Cisco OpenH264 codec. An internet connection is required for the initial download and Microsoft Store licensing. Editing your own media takes place locally on your Windows computer. A microphone is needed to record a voiceover.

Save editable projects to continue later, and keep the original photos, videos and recordings available. Project files are not a backup of your media.
