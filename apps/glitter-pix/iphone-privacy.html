Glitter Pix for iPhone: privacy

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

Glitter Pix is published by Qierah. The iPhone app has no Qierah account, advertising, tracking, analytics, chat or built-in photo-upload service.

Your pictures and projects

You choose pictures using Apple's photo picker. Editing and background removal run on your device. Photos, editable projects, recovery data, personal stickers, favourites and recent artwork choices are stored in the app's local storage. Projects and recovery data can contain copies of your pictures. Original photo files in your library are not edited by the app.

Export creates a new PNG or JPEG. Save to Photos requests permission to add your finished picture. Sharing uses Apple's share sheet; the destination you choose handles the shared file under its own privacy policy. Photos, device backups or files may be synced by Apple or another provider according to your device settings. The app does not upload pictures to Qierah or OpenAI.

Artwork and background removal

Background removal uses Apple Vision locally. Face stickers are decorations, not an identity-recognition service. Some bundled decorative illustrations were created using OpenAI image generation; using the app does not send your pictures to an image-generation service.

Purchases

Apple processes optional one-time in-app purchases. The app uses StoreKit to load localized offers and verify access to purchased packs. Qierah does not receive your card or bank details through the app. Apple handles purchase and account information under its privacy policy.

Your controls

You choose what to import, export and share. You can manage Photos permission in iPhone Settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data; exported images, shared copies and provider-managed backups can remain. Keep copies of creations you want before deleting the app. Use iPhone security and backup controls to protect local pictures and projects.

Support

If you email support, Qierah receives your address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and uses them to respond and resolve your enquiry. Do not send passwords or private pictures unless necessary for the help you request.

For support or privacy enquiries, email info@qierah.com. See the Qierah privacy policy for website analytics, email-service providers and support retention practices.
