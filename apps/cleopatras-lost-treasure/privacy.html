Cleopatra’s Lost Treasure Privacy Policy

This policy applies to Qierah’s Cleopatra’s Lost Treasure game for iPhone and Windows.

Last updated 23 November 2026

Windows edition

The Windows game saves progress, best times and scores, lives, booster credits, tutorial progress and audio settings locally in your Windows user profile. Qierah does not require a game account or receive this saved progress on its servers. Windows and backup settings can affect retention and restoration. Uninstalling or resetting the app may remove local progress and unused delivered credits.

The Windows edition uses AdsJumbo for interstitial advertising between eligible levels. AdsJumbo and advertisers may receive network information such as your IP address, device and app information, identifiers available to the SDK, and ad views or interactions to deliver and measure advertising and prevent fraud. Advertiser pages may have their own data practices. The game does not request precise location. Windows rewarded video offers are unavailable while provider support is being confirmed. For AdsJumbo information and privacy enquiries, visit AdsJumbo or contact info@adsjumbo.com.

Microsoft Store processes optional Windows booster-credit purchases and handles billing and payment details. The game checks product availability and consumable balances through Microsoft Store and keeps a local delivery record to grant credits and retry pending delivery. Qierah does not receive your payment card details. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Windows purchases and progress are separate from the iPhone edition.

Game data on your iPhone

The game saves your progress, best times and scores, lives, booster credits, tutorial progress and audio settings in its local app storage. Qierah does not require an account or receive this saved game data on its servers. Game progress stays on your device as you play. Your device backup settings may affect what Apple backs up or restores.

To remove the local game data, choose Delete App on your iPhone. Offloading the app keeps its documents and data. Deleting the game can also remove local progress and unused booster credits.

iPhone advertising through Start.io

The game uses the Start.io advertising SDK for ads between eligible levels and optional rewarded videos. The SDK can request ads in advance, even if you do not choose to watch a rewarded video. The game is configured to decline personalised ad consent through Start.io. The game does not ask for precise location or Apple tracking permission.

Start.io and its advertising partners may process information available to them, such as device or app identifiers, IP address and network details, device information, approximate location inferred from network data, and ad views, interactions or video completion. They may use this information to deliver and measure ads, detect fraud and operate their services. The information available depends on the integration and your device settings. Start.io can share information with its service providers and advertising partners as described in its policy.

In the game, open Menu and select Ad Privacy to read the Start.io End User Privacy Policy. It explains Start.io’s data handling and how to make privacy requests about data it holds.

Optional iPhone in-app purchases

Apple’s App Store processes purchases for booster credits. The game uses StoreKit to check the product and transaction and stores a purchase credit record locally to deliver the credits and avoid crediting the same transaction twice. Apple handles your account, billing and card details under its App Store privacy notice. Qierah does not receive your payment card details.

Support and privacy requests

If you email Qierah, we use your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide to respond and resolve your request. We keep support information as needed to handle the enquiry and meet applicable record-keeping requirements. For questions or to request access, correction or deletion where applicable, email info@qierah.com.

For information about the website and its hosting, see the general Qierah privacy policy. We publish updates to this app policy on this page.
